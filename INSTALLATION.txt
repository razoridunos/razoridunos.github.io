GEOCLASS iOS — RECIPIENT REBUILD, SIGNING AND INSTALLATION

Ordinary users install the original app from the App Store. The following is
for recipients exercising library-modification/relinking rights, using their
own development account, device and data. No publisher private key is needed
or supplied. This route does not publish a modified app through Geoclass's
App Store listing and does not transfer its purchase entitlements.

1. Check SHA256SUMS.txt. Read BUILD_LIBRARIES.txt and build the libraries you
   wish to change, preserving the linked interfaces/configuration. The full
   library source snapshot includes the publisher's changes. Supply matching
   Qt host tools, Apple Xcode/SDK and Google SDK separately.
2. Relink using compiled-kit/relink.py as shown in RECIPIENT_README.txt. This
   writes a new unsigned arm64 executable and result.json in a scratch folder.
3. Assemble the resources into a new unsigned bundle:
     python3 assemble_bundle.py --executable /scratch/relink/Geoclass-relinked \
       --ads /path/to/GoogleMobileAdsSdkiOS-13.11.0 --output /scratch/unsigned
   The output directory must not exist. No installed app is overwritten.
4. In your own Xcode, sign in to your Apple account. Create a temporary iOS App
   project with a separate Bundle ID you control, e.g. org.yourname.geoclassqt.
   Enable automatic development signing for your team. Connect/trust/unlock
   your device, enable Developer Mode as required, and build/run that temporary
   app once. This lets Xcode create a development certificate/profile for your
   identifier/device. Personal Team service/expiry limits apply; see Apple.
5. Locate that temporary app's embedded.mobileprovision in Xcode's build product.
   Keep it on your own Mac. List your signing certificate fingerprints:
     security find-identity -v -p codesigning
   Use the 40-character certificate SHA-1 (not a password/private key) matching
   the profile. Do not send profiles, passwords or private keys to the publisher.
6. Sign a NEW copy with the helper:
     python3 sign_recipient_bundle.py --app /scratch/unsigned/Geoclass.app \
       --profile /local/embedded.mobileprovision --identity YOUR_CERTIFICATE_SHA1 \
       --bundle-id org.yourname.geoclassqt --output /scratch/signed
   The helper verifies profile expiry, development/debug permission, identifier
   and certificate membership before copying/signing. It refuses the original
   app identifier and existing output paths. It writes no account settings and
   does not install or upload. Its --help is available without credentials.
7. Install on a device included in your profile with Xcode's Devices and
   Simulators window (+ under Installed Apps), or:
     xcrun devicectl device install app --device YOUR_DEVICE /scratch/signed/Geoclass.app
   Launch from Home Screen; trust the development identity in device Settings
   if required. Use synthetic data. The separately identified app has its own
   sandbox; it does not replace the official app or its survey data.
8. When your development profile expires, obtain a fresh profile through Xcode
   and repeat signing/installation. A free Personal Team currently has seven-day
   provisioning limits. Do not bypass code signing, reuse the publisher's App
   Store signature or jailbreak a device as part of these instructions.

SERVICE LIMITS
StoreKit products and verified purchases belong to the original Apple app
identity; a recipient's separate identity does not reproduce those entitlements.
Network ads use the original client configuration: do not generate test traffic
against live ads; start offline unless your device is properly registered for
Google test ads. Satellite is disabled without a recipient-owned credential.
These service limits must not be presented as a prohibition on modifying or
running the LGPL library portions.

VERIFICATION AND DISTRIBUTION SCOPE
The publisher checks packaged inputs, a relocated unchanged-library relink and
unsigned bundle assembly. No modified-Qt build/device QA is promised or required
by the publisher's test plan. Recipients test their modifications themselves.
The development signing helper has validation tests; no recipient certificate
or modified-library phone installation was exercised by preparing this package.
The sufficiency/applicability of installation information under LGPL 4(e) and
GPL 6, and compatibility of the actual App Store/SDK/recipient terms, remain
publisher distribution-review questions. These instructions do not certify them.
